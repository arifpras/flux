How to read this:
- Each signal day count is how many tickers met the pullback/momentum + flow regime filters that day.
- cs_mean_fwd5d is the average forward 5-day return of all signaled names on that day (not a PnL curve).
- cs_mean_fwd5d_cum is a compounded curve of those daily averages; it is not a tradeable equity curve.
- Use results/flow_augmented_momentum_picks.csv for the top-ranked tickers per day (signal_score).
- Broker flow here is market-level and undirected; no split by institutional vs retail or foreign vs domestic is available in the input.
