RESEARCH QUALITY METRICS EXPLAINED
================================================================================================================================================

Buy Return %
  = (Feb2 Price - Jan30 Price) / Jan30 Price * 100
  All negative (~-99%) because market crashed between Jan 30 and Feb 2

Sell Return % / Avoided Loss %
  = (Jan30 SellPrice - Feb2 Price) / Feb2 Price * 100
  Positive = smart exit (sold before further decline)
  Negative = poor timing (should have held)

Overall Score
  = Average of Buy Return % and Sell Return %
  Higher score = Better research quality and decision-making

Quality Rating
  • EXCELLENT ★★★: Overall Score > 0 (made smart exits despite poor buys)
  • GOOD ★★: Overall Score between -50 and 0
  • POOR ★: Overall Score < -50
