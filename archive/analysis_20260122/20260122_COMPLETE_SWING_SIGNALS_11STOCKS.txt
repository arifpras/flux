EXIT RULES - APPLY TO ALL STOCKS
================================================================================

TAKE PROFIT (Exit when ANY of these occur):

1. TARGET HIT:
   - Price reaches resistance level
   - Example: PTBA hits 2,547 → SELL ALL

2. TIME EXIT:
   - 5 days passed without reaching target
   - Reason: Opportunity cost (capital tied up)
   - Action: Exit and find new setup

3. PROFIT EXIT:
   - Any trade gains +3% or more
   - Even if target not reached, lock profit
   - Example: ASII +4% → take profit (don't be greedy)

4. STRUCTURE BREAK:
   - Price breaks above resistance significantly (+2%)
   - Set new stop at old resistance
   - Trail the stop up as price climbs


CUT LOSS (Exit IMMEDIATELY when ANY occur):

1. STOP-LOSS HIT:
   - Price touches -2% loss level
   - NO EXCEPTIONS - exit immediately
   - Do NOT move stop lower (fatal mistake)

2. SUPPORT BREAK:
   - Price breaks below support level
   - Even if not at -2% yet
   - Market structure violated = exit

3. VOLATILITY SPIKE:
   - Intraday volatility suddenly > 8%
   - Indicates panic or news event
   - Exit to preserve capital

4. FUNDAMENTAL NEWS:
   - Negative earnings, fraud, regulatory action
   - Don't wait for technical signal
   - Exit on news, regroup later


TRAILING STOP (For winning trades):

Once profit reaches +2%:
  - Move stop to breakeven (entry price)
  - Now risk-free trade
  - Let winners run to target

Once profit reaches +4%:
  - Move stop to +2% profit
  - Lock in gains
  - Continue to target
