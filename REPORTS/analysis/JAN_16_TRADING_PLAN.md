### Position Sizing

```
Risk per trade = Account × 0.5-1%
Position size = (Risk ÷ Stop Loss %) × Entry Price

Example (assuming $100K account):
- Risk per trade = $100K × 0.5% = $500
- Stop loss = 2% = 0.02
- Entry price = 100
- Shares = ($500 ÷ 0.02) ÷ 100 = 250 shares
- Max loss per trade = $500
- Target profit = 250 × 100 × 0.03 = $750
```
